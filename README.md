# Whom to support in Slovakia's growth priorities

Interactive working paper by **Tomáš Oleš** (Bratislava University of Economics and Business, Faculty of Economics and Finance), 30 October 2026.

Read it at **https://tomasoles.github.io/slovak-innovation-paper/**

The paper matches Slovakia's ten growth priorities to the patents of Slovak firms and the papers of Slovak universities and institutes by semantic similarity, maps observed collaboration (co-owned patents, co-authored papers, EU projects, the Central Register of Contracts), finds innovation clusters, and selects projects to support by explicit, tiered rules.

`index.html` is a single self-contained page: figures, data and libraries (d3, MathJax) are embedded, so it also works offline when downloaded. Every number is computed from the cited sources; none is simulated. Paper titles are shown as published; abstracts were used for matching and are not reproduced.
